BEFORE YOU PLAY

Four Golden Rules

Every player should understand these before joining.

Respect Everyone

Treat every player with respect both in-character and out-of-character.

Fair Play

No cheating, exploiting or abusing mechanics.

Quality Roleplay

Create realistic stories that improve everyone's experience.

Community First

Help create a welcoming and enjoyable environment.

ACCOUNT RULES

Protect Your Account

  • One account belongs to one player only.
  • Maximum of two characters per account.
  • Account sharing, selling or trading is prohibited.
  • No interaction between your main and alternate character.
  • Real Money Trading (RMT) is strictly prohibited.
  • Character names must be realistic.

GENERAL RULES

Respect The Community

  • Treat every player with respect.
  • English must be used during public in-character interactions.
  • Cheating, exploiting or abusing bugs is prohibited.
  • Keep OOC behaviour respectful.
  • Follow staff instructions.
  • Do not impersonate staff members.

ROLEPLAY RULES

Create Better Stories

  • No Random Deathmatch (RDM).
  • No Vehicle Deathmatch (VDM).
  • No Metagaming.
  • No Powergaming.
  • Respect New Life Rule (NLR).
  • Fear for your character's life.
  • Remain in character during roleplay.
  • Do not combat log or avoid RP.

CRIMINAL RULES

Crime With Purpose

  • Respect gang and cartel territory rules.
  • Robberies require proper roleplay.
  • Gang members must wear approved colors during official activities.
  • Follow event-specific rules during organized events.
  • Respect hostage and negotiation rules.
  • No exploiting criminal systems.

SAFE ZONES

Protected Areas

  • No shootings inside Safe Zones.
  • No robberies or kidnappings.
  • Do not use Safe Zones to escape active roleplay.
  • Continue roleplay while inside Safe Zones.
  • Respect Green Zone restrictions.
