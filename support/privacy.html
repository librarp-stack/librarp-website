Information We Collect

Libra RP collects limited information to provide, improve and secure our website, Discord community and FiveM server.

Depending on how you interact with our services, we may collect:

  • Discord username and Discord ID.
  • FiveM platform identifiers used for server moderation and account management.
  • Support requests and application information voluntarily submitted by you.
  • Basic browser and device information used for diagnostics and website performance.
  • IP address information used to detect abuse, fraud or malicious activity.

How We Use Your Information

The information we collect is used only for the operation of Libra RP and to improve your experience within our community.

  • Provide access to our FiveM server.
  • Manage department applications.
  • Respond to support requests.
  • Protect the community from abuse and cheating.
  • Improve gameplay systems and website functionality.
  • Communicate important server updates.

Discord & FiveM Services

Libra RP operates using Discord and FiveM (Cfx.re). These services may independently collect information according to their own privacy policies.

We recommend reviewing the official Privacy Policies published by Discord and Cfx.re for more information regarding their data processing practices.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember user preferences, improve performance and analyze website traffic.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings, although certain features may not function correctly.

Third-Party Services

Libra RP may rely on trusted third-party services including:

  • Discord
  • Cfx.re (FiveM)
  • Google Fonts
  • Cloudflare (if enabled)
  • Analytics providers (if implemented)

These providers maintain their own independent privacy policies.

Data Retention

Information is retained only for as long as necessary to operate the community, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes and enforce server rules.

Security

Libra RP uses reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards to protect information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration or destruction.

While no online service can guarantee absolute security, we continuously improve our infrastructure and procedures to provide a secure environment for our community.

Your Rights

Depending on applicable laws within your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Request access to information we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies upon consent.
  • Request clarification regarding how information is processed.

Children's Privacy

Libra RP is intended for users who meet the minimum age requirements established by Discord, FiveM and applicable laws. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes To This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our services, operational requirements or legal obligations.

The "Last Updated" date shown at the top of this page should always be considered the current version.

Contact

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the Libra RP Administration Team.
